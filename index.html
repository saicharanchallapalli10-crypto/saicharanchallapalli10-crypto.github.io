USB-C Inline Power Meter

Designed a pass-through USB-C power meter that measures real-time voltage, current, and power delivered to charging devices. The device sits between a USB-PD charger and a sink, such as an iPhone, and displays measurements on a 0.96" OLED.

Voltage (V) Bus voltage between the charger and device
Current (A) Current through the shunt resistor
Power (W) Voltage × current, the actual delivered power

Key design decisions

  • High-side current sensing with a 10mΩ shunt and TI INA226 over I²C.
  • Wide-input TPS62933 buck regulator for the 3.3V rail across the full USB-PD range.
  • Passive CC1/CC2 pass-through to preserve USB-PD negotiation without disrupting handshake.
  • Seeed XIAO RP2040 microcontroller for I²C control and display drive.
  • Two-layer board with ground pour, plus polyfuse and TVS diode input protection.

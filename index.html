USB-C Inline Power Meter

Completed: June 8th, 2025 Assembling: TBD

Designed a pass-through USB-C power meter that measures real-time voltage, current, and power delivered to charging devices. The goal of the PCB is to monitor power delivery to devices such as an iPhone or other USB-powered electronics while charging, operating within the USB-C Power Delivery range. The device sits between a USB-PD charger and a sink and displays measurements on a 0.96-inch OLED.

Key design decisions

  • High-side current sensing with a 10mΩ shunt and TI INA226 over I²C.
  • Wide-input TPS62933 buck regulator for the 3.3V rail across the full USB-PD range.
  • Passive CC1/CC2 pass-through to preserve USB-PD negotiation without disrupting handshake.
  • Seeed XIAO RP2040 microcontroller for I²C control and display drive.
  • Two-layer board with ground pour, plus polyfuse and TVS diode input protection.

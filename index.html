USB-C Inline Power Meter

Started: May 10th, 2025 Completed: June 8th, 2025 Assembling: TBD

I designed a pass-through USB-C power meter in KiCad that measures real-time voltage, current, and power delivered to charging devices. An INA226 performs the power calculations, and the measurements are shown on a 0.96-inch OLED while charging within the USB-C Power Delivery range. In total, the PCB I designed and manufactured measures voltage, current, and power flowing between a USB-C charger and a connected device, displaying real-time readings on a small screen.

Key design decisions

  • High-side current sensing with a 10 mOhm shunt and TI INA226 over I2C.
  • Wide input TPS62933 buck regulator for the 3.3V rail across the full USB-PD range.
  • Passive CC1/CC2 pass-through to preserve USB-PD negotiation
  • Seeed XIAO RP2040 microcontroller for I2C control and display drive.

USB-C Inline Power Meter

Started: May 10th, 2026 Completed: June 8th, 2026 Assembly in progress!

I designed a pass-through USB-C power meter in KiCad that measures voltage, current, and power delivered to charging devices. From schematic to the full pcb layout/routing, I deicded to take on this project to learn more about how a USB-C power delivery system works and how to design a PCB that can handle the power requirements of USB-C devices; Also I oringally wanted to buy a USB-C power meter to test my USB-C devices for fun, but I thought to myself "Why not design my own and learn something new in the process?" From a highly implified technical perspective, a power monitor performs the power calculations, and the measurements are shown on a 0.96-inch screenwhile charging within the USB-C Power Delivery range.

Key design decisions

  • High-side current sensing with a 10 mOhm shunt and TI INA226 over I2C.
  • Wide input TPS62933 buck regulator for the 3.3V rail across the full USB-PD range.
  • Passive CC1/CC2 pass-through to preserve USB-PD negotiation
  • Seeed XIAO RP2040 microcontroller for I2C control and display drive.
